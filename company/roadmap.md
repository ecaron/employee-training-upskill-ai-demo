# Fancy Hats, Inc. — Strategic Roadmap

*Vision: Be the first name in hats nationwide by 2028.*

---

## Year 1 — 2026: Build the Engine
**Theme:** Get the house in order before scaling.

### Goals
- Modern e-commerce platform live and stable
- Denver pop-up validates West Coast demand
- Omnichannel customer service launched
- Forecasting system live across all categories

### Targets
- Revenue: **$50M** (+19%)
- E-commerce share: **28%** of revenue
- NPS: **maintain 75+**
- New customers: **+25%**

### Key Bets
- Replatform e-comm (foundation for everything else)
- First step west: Denver
- Begin millennial brand refresh

---

## Year 2 — 2027: Cross the Mississippi
**Theme:** Prove we can win outside our home turf.

### Goals
- 4 new permanent stores: Denver, Austin, Nashville, Chicago
- E-commerce ships from 2 fulfillment centers (East + Central)
- Brand refresh fully rolled out
- Launch Fancy Hats mobile app

### Targets
- Revenue: **$65M** (+30%)
- E-commerce share: **35%**
- New customer share from west of Mississippi: **20%**
- Repeat rate: **50%**

### Key Bets
- Aggressive but disciplined retail expansion (4 stores, not 14)
- National brand campaign tied to refresh
- Loyalty program launch

---

## Year 3 — 2028: Coast to Coast
**Theme:** First name in hats, nationwide.

### Goals
- 25+ retail locations spanning all major US regions
- West Coast fulfillment center live (3 total) — 2-day shipping nationwide
- Recognized as the leading hat brand in the US (unaided awareness)
- Sustainable, profitable growth — no death-march expansion

### Targets
- Revenue: **$90M** (+38%)
- E-commerce share: **40%**
- Stores in **15+ states**
- NPS: **80+**
- Profitable every quarter

### Key Bets
- LA, Seattle, San Francisco flagships
- Private-label premium line
- International toe-dip (Canada, UK e-comm)

---

## Guiding Principles
1. **Service is the product.** Don't scale past our ability to deliver it.
2. **Profitable growth or no growth.** No vanity expansion.
3. **Curate, don't proliferate.** SKU count grows slowly and deliberately.
4. **Heritage is leverage, not a cage.** 100 years of trust — use it, don't hide behind it.
