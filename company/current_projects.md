# Fancy Hats, Inc. — Current Projects

*As of Q2 2026 — projects actively in flight, owned, and funded.*

## Active Projects

### 1. E-Commerce Replatform
- **Owner:** Priya Ramanathan (CTO)
- **Goal:** Replace 2019 Magento build with a modern headless stack; cut page load by 50%, enable real-time inventory.
- **Status:** 🟡 In dev — soft launch July 2026
- **Why it matters:** E-comm is our fastest-growing channel; current site is the bottleneck.

### 2. Western Expansion — Phase 1 (Denver Pop-Up)
- **Owner:** Eleanor Park (VP Merch)
- **Goal:** 90-day pop-up in Denver to test West Coast demand and brand awareness ahead of permanent store.
- **Status:** 🟢 On track — opens June 2026
- **Why it matters:** First physical presence west of the Mississippi. Real data for the 3-year plan.

### 3. Customer Service Modernization
- **Owner:** Tomás Reyes (VP CX)
- **Goal:** Move from email + phone to omnichannel (chat, SMS, social) without sacrificing the "3-ring" promise.
- **Status:** 🟢 Vendor selected, rollout Q3 2026
- **Why it matters:** Our service is the brand. We can't scale nationally on a 2010 helpdesk.

### 4. Inventory & Demand Forecasting Overhaul
- **Owner:** David Chen (COO)
- **Goal:** Replace spreadsheets with a forecasting system; reduce stockouts by 30%, deadstock by 20%.
- **Status:** 🟡 Pilot running on summer collection
- **Why it matters:** Stockouts of bestsellers cost ~$2M last year.

### 5. Millennial Brand Refresh
- **Owner:** Margaret Holloway (CEO) + outside agency
- **Goal:** Refresh visual identity, packaging, and digital storytelling for a younger buyer — without alienating the core customer.
- **Status:** 🟡 Concepts in review
- **Why it matters:** Core customer is aging. We need the next generation now.

### 6. Hatmakers' Apprentice Program
- **Owner:** Eleanor Park
- **Goal:** Partner with 3 design schools to train next-gen milliners; secure exclusive emerging-designer collections.
- **Status:** 🟢 First cohort starts fall 2026
- **Why it matters:** Differentiation through unique product.
