# Fancy Hats, Inc. — Company Dossier

## At a Glance
- **Founded:** 1925 (100 years)
- **HQ:** Boston, MA
- **Employees:** 180
- **Revenue:** $42M (FY24)
- **Stage:** Privately held, profitable, growth mode

## What We Do
We sell the finest hats for men and women — fedoras, panamas, sun hats, derbies, cloches, and custom commissions — through our flagship store, regional boutiques, and a growing e-commerce site.

## Mission
Deliver the best hat-buying experience in America. Period.

## Vision
Be the first name in hats nationwide by 2028.

## Where We Sell
- 12 boutique retail locations (Boston, NYC, Philadelphia, DC, Charleston, Atlanta)
- fancyhats.dev (launched 2019, ~22% of revenue)
- Wholesale partnerships with 40+ specialty retailers

## Customer
- Core: 35–65, household income $90K+, values craftsmanship
- Growth: 25–40 millennials/Gen-Z rediscovering classic style
- Geographic: 80% east of the Mississippi (today)

## What Makes Us Different
- **Service obsession.** Free shipping both ways. 365-day returns. Phone answered in 3 rings.
- **Curation over assortment.** We carry 600 SKUs, not 6,000.
- **Real expertise.** Every customer-facing employee passes a 4-week hat fitting certification.
- **Heritage.** Four generations of the founding family still involved.

## Financial Snapshot
- Gross margin: 58%
- YoY revenue growth: 19%
- E-commerce growth: 41%
- Customer repeat rate: 44%
- NPS: 78

## Key Risks
- Geographic concentration in the East
- Aging core customer base
- Legacy ERP and inventory systems holding back e-commerce
- Heavy reliance on seasonal (spring/summer) demand
