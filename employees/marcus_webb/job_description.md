# Job Description — Customer Service Team Lead

**Reports to:** VP Customer Experience
**Department:** Customer Experience
**Location:** Boston HQ
**Level:** People manager (M1)

---

## Purpose
Lead the front-line customer service team that delivers Fancy Hats' signature "3-ring" service experience across phone, email, chat, and SMS.

## Core Responsibilities
- Manage a team of 8–12 customer service representatives
- Hit and maintain SLAs: 3-ring answer rate, first-contact resolution, CSAT
- Coach reps through 1:1s, call reviews, and quarterly performance reviews
- Own scheduling, capacity planning, and seasonal staffing
- Partner with E-Comm, Retail, and Operations on customer issues and process fixes
- Serve as power user for CX platform; surface data insights to leadership

## Required Qualifications
- 3+ years in customer service, including 1+ leading people
- Track record hitting CSAT and SLA targets
- Strong written and verbal communication

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with omnichannel CX platforms (Gladly, Kustomer, Zendesk)
- Comfort with data analysis and reporting tools
- Background in retail or DTC

## Success Metrics
- 3-ring answer rate ≥ 95%
- CSAT ≥ 4.7/5.0
- First-contact resolution ≥ 80%
- Team retention
