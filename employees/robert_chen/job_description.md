# Job Description — Store Manager, Boston Flagship

**Reports to:** Director of Retail
**Department:** Retail Operations
**Location:** Boston, MA (Flagship)
**Level:** People manager (M2)

---

## Purpose
Own the performance, team, and customer experience of Fancy Hats' largest and most visible retail location.

## Core Responsibilities
- Deliver store revenue, margin, and labor targets
- Hire, develop, and retain a team of 12–15
- Maintain the brand-defining in-store experience: visual merch, fitting service, events
- Plan and execute in-store events (trunk shows, designer visits, seasonal launches)
- Partner with Buying, Marketing, and Operations on store-specific needs
- Serve as a sounding board for new initiatives — flagship is often pilot site

## Required Qualifications
- 5+ years retail management; 2+ at store manager level
- P&L ownership experience
- Demonstrated team leadership and development

## Preferred Qualifications
- Specialty or premium retail background
- Event planning experience
- Multi-store or new-store opening experience

## Success Metrics
- Store revenue vs. plan
- 4-wall margin
- NPS / customer feedback
- Team retention and internal promotion rate
