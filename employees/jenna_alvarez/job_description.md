# Job Description — E-Commerce Coordinator

**Reports to:** Director of E-Commerce
**Department:** E-Commerce / Digital
**Location:** Boston HQ
**Level:** Individual contributor (IC2)

---

## Purpose
Keep fancyhats.dev running smoothly day-to-day — merchandising, content, and the operational glue between Merch, Marketing, and Engineering.

## Core Responsibilities
- Execute weekly site merchandising updates (homepage, collections, promos)
- Coordinate product launches end-to-end: copy, photography, PDP setup, QA
- Maintain the site content calendar and promotional schedule
- Pull and share weekly performance reports (traffic, conversion, AOV)
- Triage site issues and coordinate fixes with engineering
- Support major projects (e.g., replatform, mobile app, fulfillment expansion)

## Required Qualifications
- 1+ years in e-commerce, digital marketing, or related role
- Familiarity with at least one e-commerce platform (Magento, Shopify, etc.)
- Strong attention to detail; comfort working across teams

## Preferred Qualifications
- Basic HTML/CSS
- Email marketing platform experience (Klaviyo, Braze)
- Analytics fluency (GA4)

## Success Metrics
- On-time launch rate for product drops
- Site error/issue rate
- Conversion rate trend on managed pages
