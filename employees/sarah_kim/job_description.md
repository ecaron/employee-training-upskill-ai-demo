# Job Description — Assistant Buyer, Women's Collection

**Reports to:** Senior Buyer, Women's
**Department:** Merchandising
**Location:** Boston HQ
**Level:** Individual contributor (IC2)

---

## Purpose
Support the Women's buying team in selecting, planning, and managing the seasonal hat assortment that drives Fancy Hats' women's revenue across retail and e-commerce.

## Core Responsibilities
- Manage open-to-buy budget for assigned categories ($3–5M)
- Build and maintain seasonal assortment plans in partnership with Senior Buyer
- Negotiate costs, MOQs, and delivery terms with vendors
- Analyze sell-through, margin, and stockout data; recommend reorders and markdowns
- Attend market weeks and trade shows; identify new vendors and trends
- Partner with Planning, E-Comm, and Marketing on product launches

## Required Qualifications
- 2+ years in retail buying, planning, or merchandising
- Strong retail math, advanced Excel
- Demonstrated vendor management experience

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience in specialty or premium retail
- Familiarity with assortment planning tools
- Sketching or design literacy

## Success Metrics
- Sell-through % vs. target
- Gross margin % vs. plan
- Vendor on-time delivery
- Stockout rate on key items
